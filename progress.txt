## Iteration v21.4
- **Status:** COMPLETED
- **Learnings:**
  - The `auth.role()` call in Supabase RLS policies caused major performance warnings. Solution: Wrap in `(SELECT auth.role())`.
  - Scalping requires sub-5s loops. We reduced the `autonomous_hunter_loop` sleep from 10s to 3s and saw immediate responsiveness.
  - Hardcoded capital limits ($20) inhibit scalability. We switched to `get_compounded_amount()` using real Binance balance.
  - Institutional flow is invisible to RSI/MACD. We implemented `Volume Spike Detection` (>80% avg) to track whales.
  - Vercel/Render communication requires strictly configured CORS. We hardened `cloud_api.py` to only allow specific origins.
